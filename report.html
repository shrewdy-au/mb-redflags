MB Red Flags

A GO / INVESTIGATE / NO-GO panel for City of Sydney listings, built on the Microburbs API. Microburbs hackathon, 7 Nov 2026. Team MOGL (Jake Milne).

1. Data

  • Microburbs API, City of Sydney LGA: geocode/address, properties/{gnaf}/profile, risks/all, surroundings/public-housing, street/profile, valuation/avm, valuation/agent-quoting, transport/nearby, suburbs/{SAL}/risks/extras, suburbs/{SAL}/risks/property-hazard-counts.
  • From the listing page: the address only. The asking price is typed in by the user.
  • Held-out check: the supplied public-housing CSV and the supplied address list, used on the day only and not republished.

2. Method

  1. Resolve the address to a G-NAF id. Choose the geocode candidate whose abbr matches the street number and street name. For units, try 3/12 Smith St first, then Unit 3 12 Smith St.
  2. Make 8 tier-1 calls with Promise.allSettled, at most 4 at a time.
  3. Mesh-block ANEF join: profile.mesh_block → risks/extras.per_mb.risks[].anef_med. Labelled "microburb estimate, not a property contour". Falls back to the suburb share.
  4. On-property vs distance: each hazard gets its own buffer. Public housing is measured twice, as the area share and as the 200 m share, and the row goes red if the subject address itself is flagged.
  5. Street forecast rank, compared with the suburb median. Thin evidence is greyed out, not rated.
  6. Any RED → NO-GO, any AMBER → INVESTIGATE, otherwise GO. The wording is neutral: decision support, not advice.
